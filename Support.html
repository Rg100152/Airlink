Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my flight booking?
You can cancel your booking through the "My Bookings" section on our website or mobile app. Cancellation charges apply based on the fare type and time of cancellation.
What is the baggage allowance?
For domestic flights, Economy class passengers are allowed 15kg check-in baggage and 7kg cabin baggage. Business class allows 25kg check-in and 7kg cabin.
How can I web check-in?
Web check-in opens 48 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes prior. You can check in via our website or app and download your boarding pass.
When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are processed within 7-10 working days to the original payment method. For credit cards, it may take up to 14 days depending on your bank.
Can I change my flight date?
Yes, date changes are allowed subject to availability and applicable change fees. You can modify your booking through the "Manage Booking" section.
