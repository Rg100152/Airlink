About AIRLINK

Connecting India to the world, one flight at a time. Discover our story, our mission, and the people behind the wings.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, AIRLINK began with a simple vision: to make air travel across India accessible, affordable, and enjoyable for every citizen. What started as a small regional carrier with just three aircraft has now grown into one of the country's most trusted domestic airlines.

Over the past decade, we have expanded our network to over 45 destinations, connecting major metros like Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore to the hidden gems of the Northeast and the serene beaches of the South. We take pride in our Indian heritage and strive to reflect the warmth and hospitality of our nation in every flight we operate.

Today, AIRLINK is not just an airline; it is a bridge that connects families, businesses, and cultures across the length and breadth of Incredible India.
